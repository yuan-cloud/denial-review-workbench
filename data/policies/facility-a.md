# Facility A — Prior Authorization Policy

## Skilled Nursing Follow-up

Required documentation for authorization:
- Signed physician order within 30 days of service
- Progress notes within 30 days of service
- Functional assessment if last assessment older than 60 days

Denial appeal basis:
- Missing documents: request specific missing items, then resubmit
- Medical necessity dispute: attach clinical notes with physician
  attestation before filing formal appeal
- Conflicting denial reasons: escalate to compliance review before acting

Approval threshold: all required documents present and dated correctly.
